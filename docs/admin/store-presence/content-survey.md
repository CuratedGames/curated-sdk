# Content Survey

**Store Presence > Content Survey** describes general content, mature topics, and generative AI
use. You must complete it before you can mark Store Presence ready or submit for review.

The survey walks through sections (violence, language, drugs, nudity, and more). Two free text
answers matter most:

- **What should customers know about any mature content?** Shown on the store page.
- **How do we access the mature content in your game?** Only Curated reviewers see this.

The final tab lists anything left to fix. Past versions are under **View content survey
history**.
