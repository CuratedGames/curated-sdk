# Publish and player reviews

## Publish

**Store Presence > Publish** lists the remaining checks. When the content survey is complete,
click **Mark Store Presence ready**. Then submit from [Release](../release).

## Player reviews

**Store Presence > Player reviews** lists reviews of your game. Post one public response per
review; you can edit it anytime. Players see it attributed to your organization. If the game is
on Steam, **Import Steam reviews** brings existing reviews over.
