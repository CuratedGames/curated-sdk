# Pricing

**Store Presence > Pricing** sets what players pay on first purchase.

## Primary store price

Enter the **Primary store price (USD)** and click **Save price**.

**Hide price until the release date** keeps the price off the store until release. It needs an
intended release date first, set in [Release](../release).

## Regional prices

Tick **Use automatic regional pricing** to fill other currencies from your USD price. Curated
weighs purchasing power, comparable entertainment costs, and exchange rates. You can still
override any currency: type a price and **Save**, or go back with **Use suggested**. After you
change the USD price, click **Refresh from base USD price**.

## Resale

Resale Market settings are not here. See [Resales and paid reviews](../visibility-sales).
