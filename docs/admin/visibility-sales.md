# Resales and paid reviews

**Improve visibility and sales** has two tabs.

## Resales

When a player resells your game on the Resale Market, the listing price is split 50% to the
seller, 40% to you, and 10% to Curated. The cooldown resets for every new owner. Free games
cannot be resold.

- **Allow Resale Market listings for this product**.
- **Max discount % off store price**: how far below the live store price listings can go.
- **Resale cooldown (days)**: how long a new owner waits before reselling.

Click **Save resale settings**.

## Paid reviews

Offer a cashback when players review the game within a set number of days after getting it
(purchase or resale). The cashback is a percent of what that player paid and comes from your
share. Set **Cashback percent** to 0 to turn it off. Players get it once per game.
