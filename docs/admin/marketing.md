# Marketing

**Marketing** shows where players discover your store page.

- **Store & Platform Traffic**: impressions, visits, and click-through by source and platform.
- **UTM Analytics**: visits, wishlists, purchases, and key activations within 72 hours of a
  campaign link. Add `utm_source` or `utm_campaign` (also supported: `utm_medium`,
  `utm_content`, `utm_term`) to links to your store page.
- **Visibility Rounds**: how often the game appears on store surfaces, and how often that leads
  to visits.
- **Widget**: embed code for a store widget on your site. Its links include
  `utm_source=storewidget`.
- **Wishlists**: daily wishlist additions, deletions, purchases, gifts, and resales.
